Peeling Paint or Window Dust: What Service Do You Need?

Peeling paint is a reason to investigate, not proof of lead. In a pre-1978 home, stop the disturbance and choose the service that answers your actual question.

I need to know whether the paint contains lead

Ask about a lead paint inspection. It locates lead-based paint but does not by itself evaluate every exposure hazard. Bring renovation plans so the inspector knows which components are in question.

I need to know whether my home has hazards

Ask about a lead risk assessment when deteriorated paint, dust, soil or possible exposure is the concern. Explain where children spend time and when the condition first appeared. Follow medical guidance for any exposure concern.

I am about to replace windows or remodel

Discuss testing and the qualifications of the renovation firm before demolition. EPA's RRP guidance covers paid disturbance of paint in applicable pre-1978 properties. Abatement has a different purpose and certification structure.

Common questions

Can I tell lead paint by looking at it?

No. Color, texture and the age of a house cannot answer the question. Only testing confirms whether a coating contains lead.

Should I scrape off a sample myself?

No. Disturbing suspect paint can create the dust problem you are trying to investigate. Stop the disturbance and ask a professional about testing.
