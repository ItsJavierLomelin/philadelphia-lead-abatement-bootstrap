Lead Abatement vs Lead-Safe Renovation

The same wall or window may be involved in very different projects. The purpose of the work helps determine which lead program applies.

Abatement permanently addresses a hazard

EPA defines abatement as activity designed to permanently eliminate lead-based paint hazards. Pennsylvania certifies lead abatement contractors through the Department of Labor and Industry. Verify the firm and the individual qualifications for inspection, risk assessment or abatement.

Renovation changes the building

EPA's Renovation, Repair and Painting program covers paid work that disturbs paint in applicable older housing and child-occupied facilities. A kitchen remodel or replacement window project is not automatically abatement just because lead-safe practices are necessary.

Put the distinction in the proposal

Ask the professional to name the project type, certifications, methods and final documents. An RRP certificate alone is not proof of abatement qualifications. For a commercial-only building, other worker-safety rules may apply even where a residential lead rule does not.

Common questions

Is an RRP-certified renovation firm an abatement contractor?

Not automatically. An EPA renovation certificate is not interchangeable with Pennsylvania abatement or risk-assessment certifications. Verify the credential for the actual task.

Which one should I ask for?

Start from the purpose. Permanent elimination of a documented hazard points to abatement; changing the building while managing paint safely points to lead-safe renovation.
