Rental Lead Certification for Philadelphia Property Owners

If a tenant, agent or licensing step asks for lead paperwork, start with the exact requirement in writing. Philadelphia ties rental licenses and new leases to lead certification for pre-1978 properties, and the document has to come from the right kind of professional.

Check the current Philadelphia requirement

The City's lead paint regulations page explains that owners of pre-1978 rentals must give tenants a certification, prepared by a dust wipe technician, that the property is lead-safe or lead-free in order to execute a lease or hold a rental license. Confirm the current rules for your property rather than relying on an old checklist.

The certification is only one obligation

A lead-safe certification does not erase requirements tied to paid renovation, abatement, a health order or a federally assisted housing program. Keep the reason for the lead work separate from the rental paperwork, and keep the certification current before it expires under the city's timeline.

Bring the actual notice

Have the property address, unit count, building age, deadline and any city correspondence ready. Ask which report is needed, who may prepare it and how it is submitted. Only a properly certified professional can produce the certification the city accepts, and no private provider can guarantee a licensing outcome.

Common questions

What does Philadelphia require before I sign a lease?

For pre-1978 rentals, the city requires a certification, prepared by a dust wipe technician, that the property is lead-safe or lead-free in order to execute a lease or hold a rental license. Confirm the current rules for your property.

Can any contractor prepare the certification?

No. The city specifies the kind of certified professional who prepares it. A private provider also cannot guarantee a licensing outcome, so treat any such promise as a warning sign.

Does the certification cover renovation rules?

No. Paid renovation, abatement, health orders and housing-program obligations each carry their own requirements, separate from the rental certification.
