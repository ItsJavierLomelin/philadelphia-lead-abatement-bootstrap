Germantown: lead paint planning in one of America's oldest neighborhoods

Germantown has been building and rebuilding since 1683. Its houses carry original trim, wavy glass, deep porches and layers of paint from three centuries. Planning lead work here means protecting the details that make the neighborhood itself.

A historic name is not a diagnosis

The Encyclopedia of Greater Philadelphia calls Germantown one of America's most historic neighborhoods, with sites documented by the Historic American Buildings Survey. That context justifies checking painted components early. It does not prove that any particular surface contains lead. Testing does that.

List the components before choosing methods

Sash, frame, porch rail, exterior trim, interior doors: write down what the project will actually touch. A report that maps findings to that list keeps the scope honest. Different components on the same house can need different solutions.

Preservation approval is its own track

For a designated historic property, exterior changes may need Philadelphia Historical Commission review before components are ordered or removed. A lead plan does not grant preservation approval, and an approval does not address lead. Run both tracks in the same written scope so they do not collide mid-job.

Compare methods honestly

EPA identifies removal, special sealing and enclosure among abatement methods. Ordinary repainting is not permanent abatement, and a coating cannot fix unrelated dust or soil hazards. Ask what the chosen method leaves behind and what later maintenance must avoid.

Keep the record for the next owner

File the inspection, the scope, photos of existing conditions and the final documentation together. Do not call a house lead-free on the strength of a neat finish. Ask precisely what the evidence establishes and keep it where the next project can find it.

Common questions

Does historic designation change lead work?

It adds a separate approval track. For a designated property, discuss exterior changes with the Philadelphia Historical Commission before components are ordered or removed. A lead plan does not grant preservation approval.

Is encapsulation allowed on historic trim?

Suitability depends on the surface and the project. Ask what the method leaves behind and what later maintenance or renovation must avoid.
