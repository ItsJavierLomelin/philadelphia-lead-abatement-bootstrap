Common questions

The porch railing paint is chipping onto the walkway. What should I do first?

Keep people, especially children, away from the flaking area and ask about testing. Scraping or pressure washing first can spread contamination further.

Can I repaint over old paint to seal it?

Ordinary wall paint is not an abatement system. Ask whether the surface suits encapsulation, enclosure or another method, and get the answer in writing.
