A mill borough beside new towers

The Conshohocken Historical Society describes a Schuylkill River borough settled about 1820 as Matson's Ford and later home to firms like the Alan Wood Iron and Steel Company.

Mill-era housing was built for workers and painted for a century afterward. Stair rails and balusters get touched daily and repainted rarely, which makes failing paint on them a different conversation from a peeling bedroom wall.

Common questions

The paint on our stair rail is wearing through. Is that urgent?

Treat it as a testing question, especially with children in the house. Avoid sanding it smooth, keep hands washed and ask for the rail to be included in the tested components.

Do I need a full-house inspection for one bad railing?

Not necessarily, but a single-component answer still comes from testing. Describe exactly which surfaces concern you and ask for findings by component.
