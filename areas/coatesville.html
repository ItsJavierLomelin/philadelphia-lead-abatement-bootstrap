Common questions

Several windows are painted shut. Should I force them open?

Forcing painted joints can make dust and chips. Have the paint tested first; if lead is present, a controlled approach protects the room while the windows are addressed.

Is a window-only project still lead work?

Yes, if the coatings contain lead. Paid disturbance of lead paint in applicable older housing carries lead-safe practice requirements, whatever the project size.
